Event Information
Everything you need to know
⛳ Golf Format
  • Individual Stableford on both days at 95% Playing Handicap
  • Men play White tees · Ladies play Red tees
  • Team Stableford: best 2 Stableford points from the fourball counted on each hole
  • Each day: Individual 1st/2nd, Ladies Winner, Winning Fourball, 2 × NP + 1 × NP2
  • Overall 36-hole: 1st / 2nd / 3rd
📋 Cards & Scoring
  • Players labelled A–D by ascending Handicap Index — Player A is the Fourball Captain
  • Individual scorecards are swapped and marked in pairs
  • Player A maintains the master A/B/C/D card; all 5 cards are cross-checked and returned together
  • HI / Course Handicap / Playing Handicap are organiser-checked before each round
🏨 Stay & Logistics
  • All 56 golfers stay Monday night
  • Progressive Monday check-in as fourballs finish
  • Tuesday: check out before golf. Secure luggage storage with Hanbury is optional
  • Day 2 starter sheets published on Monday evening
🎩 Dress Code
  • Golf attire during the day
  • Smart casual for the Captain's Dinner
